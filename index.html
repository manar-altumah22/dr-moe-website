Our Story

A doctor's understanding of skin, in a calm and private studio.

Dr. Moe founded the studio with one idea: skin care should be guided by real medical knowledge, not trends. Every facial and treatment is planned around how your skin actually works.

Dr. Moe earned his Doctor of Medicine from Georgian American University School of Medicine and has worked in Canadian healthcare as a Physician Assistant, including in walk-in and family practice. He then focused his career on skin health, completing the Advanced Medical Esthetics Practitioner program at St. Clair College in Windsor with a perfect 4.0 GPA and a Certificate of Academic Distinction.

He sees the skin as an organ, not just a surface. It protects the body, regulates temperature and reflects overall health, so stress, sleep and hormones all play a part. His approach combines medical knowledge, education and realistic expectations: gentle care that protects the skin barrier, no unnecessary treatments, and results that last.

FAQ

Good to know

Do you treat men?

Yes. Our facials and skin treatments are for men and women. Men often come in for deep cleansing, acne and ingrown hairs, razor irritation, oily skin and dull, tired-looking skin. Every treatment is adjusted to your skin type.

Do you offer Botox or fillers?

No. The studio focuses only on skin treatments: facials, chemical peels, dermaplaning, microdermabrasion, microneedling, red light therapy and acne care. We do not offer injectables of any kind.

Which treatment should I book first?

Start with the Signature Facial. It includes a full skin analysis, and Dr. Moe will recommend what to do next.

Is there downtime after a peel?

Most light peels have little to no downtime. Some light flaking may happen for a few days. You'll get aftercare instructions at your visit.

How often should I get a facial?

Every 4 to 6 weeks works for most skin, matching your skin's natural renewal cycle. Acne programs may start more often.

Can I be treated by a female esthetician?

Yes. A female member of our team is available on request. Choose it in the booking form or mention it when you call.

Is Dr. Moe a real doctor?

Dr. Moe holds a Doctor of Medicine degree from Georgian American University School of Medicine and is a former Physician Assistant in Canada. He also graduated with distinction from the Advanced Medical Esthetics Practitioner program at St. Clair College and has more than five years of experience in skin care. He owns the studio and leads every new client's consultation.

What languages do you speak?

Dr. Moe speaks Arabic, English and Turkish. Let us know when you book which language you'd like for your consultation.

Where are you located?

We are in Windsor, Ontario, and see clients by appointment only. The full address will be provided once your booking is confirmed.

What are your hours?

Monday to Friday, 7:00 AM to 10:00 PM. Saturday and Sunday appointments are available by reservation. Call (226) 506-3772 to book.

How can I pay?

We accept debit, Visa, Mastercard, Apple Pay and Google Pay, as well as Interac e-Transfer. The balance is paid after your treatment.

Do I need to pay a deposit?

Yes. A $25 deposit secures your appointment. Once we confirm your time on WhatsApp or by text, we'll send you a secure payment link, or you can pay by Interac e-Transfer to Williamalghanim@gmail.com (Autodeposit, no password needed). The deposit is taken off your total on the day.

Will I get a receipt?

Yes. You'll receive a receipt by text or email for your deposit and for your final payment, showing the treatment, date and amount.

What is your cancellation policy?

Please give at least 24 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule. With 24 hours' notice, your deposit can be moved to a new appointment or refunded. Late cancellations and missed appointments forfeit the deposit.
